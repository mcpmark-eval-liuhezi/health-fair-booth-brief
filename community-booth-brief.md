## 🔬 Research Corner: Eli Lilly Head-to-Head Study (NCT05822830)

**Title:** A Phase 3b, Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Compared to Semaglutide in Adults Who Have Obesity or Overweight With Weight Related Comorbidities

- **Current recruitment status (as reported on clinicaltrials.gov today):** COMPLETED
- **Sponsor:** Eli Lilly and Company
- **Phase:** Phase 3b
- **Design:** Randomized controlled; ~74-week tirzepatide vs. semaglutide head-to-head in adults with obesity/overweight + weight-related comorbidities (without Type 2 Diabetes)
- **Talking point for visitors:** This study is now **closed to new enrollment (completed)**. It's a great hook to explain how we now have head-to-head comparative data on two leading weight-management medications — and it validates why we keep asking: *"Is there a research opportunity for you right now?"*
